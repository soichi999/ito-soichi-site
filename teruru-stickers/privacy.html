Summary

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, does not use the internet, and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

How the app works

All sticker images are included inside the app. When you tap “Add to WhatsApp”, the sticker images are passed to the WhatsApp app on your own device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. How WhatsApp handles stickers is covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
